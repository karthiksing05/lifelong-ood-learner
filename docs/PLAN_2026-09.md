Plan restructured 2026-09-25 (late) on the user's redirect: "shoot for agentic AI right now; cancel everything
that's not embodied; continual learning for diffusion policy via the scheme we're working on; build off
Zekun's ICLR 2027 submission; focus on isolating agent prototypes and finding ways to do composition."
The image phases are paused (their state is in Appendix B); everything below is the embodied line.

## 1. Goal, thesis and claims

**Goal.** A diffusion policy that keeps learning tasks for its whole life, where a new task is first
COMPOSED from the agent's existing prototypes and only what no prototype explains is FOLDED into the
policy, at a measured cost in bytes, without damaging earlier tasks.

**Thesis (embodied form).** A pretrained diffusion policy already holds a hierarchy of *agent prototypes*:
the modes of its noisy action-chunk marginals, one hierarchy level per noise level (coarse = the gross
motion, fine = the precise path), exactly as Zekun's paper shows for images (modes of p_t reached by
score ascent, distinct at fine levels, merging as the level coarsens). A new task is a composition of
those prototypes selected by observation; lifelong learning is the incremental, Cobweb-like growth of
that library: decompose, address, fold, consolidate.

**Claims the paper makes.**
1. Agent prototypes exist and are isolable: score ascent on a trained policy's action denoiser recovers
   discrete, recurring motion prototypes (reach / grasp / lift / carry / release and their variants),
   organised by noise level, that recur ACROSS tasks (the incidence of prototypes over tasks is sparse
   and shared, not one-per-task).
2. Composition is the mechanism of transfer: a held-out task can be executed from ONE demonstration by
   decomposing its chunks into known prototypes and sampling the policy through their product of
   experts, with success far above zero-shot conditioning and near few-shot fine-tuning, at zero weights.
3. Folding is the mechanism of acquisition: a task that needs a motion no prototype explains is learned
   by a responsibility-gated minimal expert on the action denoiser, anchored on the policy's own samples
   of the other prototypes, and later tasks reuse it; cost tracks the vocabulary of motions, not the
   number of tasks.
4. Hierarchy and sparsity are load-bearing: the depth at which a new motion is distinct predicts what it
   costs to fold; a dense or parameter-sparse edit destroys old skills where a gated one does not.
5. The same operators are Cobweb's (categorise / incorporate / create / merge), and the correspondence
   proved for images (the ideal denoiser's one-instance update IS Cobweb's, noise level = depth,
   docs/COBWEB_UPDATE.md) holds for action chunks unchanged.

The Zekun paper's stated limitation ("detecting, consolidating and reusing genuinely new primitives") is
claims 3-4; its method is inherited unchanged as the discovery and the composition operator.

## 3. Substrate: the Physical Intelligence models first, a plain Diffusion Policy as the control

User's addition (2026-09-25, late): "Focus on the Physical Intelligence models as well: pi0.5 and pi0.7."

- **Primary prior: pi0.5 (Physical Intelligence, open weights via openpi).** A PaliGemma-3B VLM with a
  ~300M action expert trained by FLOW MATCHING over an action chunk, conditioned on images, proprioception
  and the task instruction; released checkpoints include a LIBERO fine-tune (pi05_libero) with a LIBERO
  evaluation example and the LIBERO demos in LeRobot format on the Hub. This is the "evolutionary prior"
  of the user's notes made literal: a foundation policy pretrained on many robots, then fine-tuned. Our
  operators attach to the ACTION EXPERT only, which is also where pi0.5's knowledge insulation keeps
  gradients (the VLM is insulated from the action loss), so a fold = a gated expert inside the action
  expert, the VLM untouched. pi0.7, if it exists and is open by now, replaces or joins pi0.5; pi0.6
  (RECAP) is the RL-refined line and is cited (Appendix A settles what is public).
- **Score from the velocity field (verified in openpi's code).** openpi trains v on the path
  x_t = t * eps + (1 - t) * a with target u_t = eps - a, t ~ Beta(1.5, 1) * 0.999 + 0.001 (t = 1 is noise,
  t = 0 is data), and samples by Euler x <- x + dt * v with dt = -1/10 (pi0.py compute_loss / sample_actions).
  Since v = E[eps - a | x_t] and eps = (x_t - (1 - t) a) / t, one gets E[a | x_t] = x_t - t * v (Tweedie),
  E[eps | x_t] = x_t + (1 - t) * v, and grad log p_t(x_t) = -E[eps | x_t] / t = -(x_t + (1 - t) v) / t
  (the noise std at t is t). So the PolicyBackbone exposes eps_hat = x_t + (1 - t) v and score = -eps_hat / t,
  Zekun's mode ascent (x <- x + eta * score, Tweedie mode a = x_t - t v), the Laplace extent (Hutchinson
  on the score) and the PoE guide (Eq. 14 written for this path: the diffused Gaussian of a prototype
  N(m, S) at t is N((1 - t) m, (1 - t)^2 S + t^2 I)) all apply; the sampler integrates the flow with the
  guide mixed into v. pi05_libero: action horizon 10, action dim 32 (7 used, zero-padded), 30k steps at
  batch 256, EMA 0.999, published 98.8 / 98.2 / 98.0 / 92.4 on Spatial / Object / Goal / 10.
- **Control substrate: a plain Diffusion Policy** (Chi et al. 2023; U-Net or transformer denoiser, DDPM
  cosine schedule, 16-step chunk, 8 executed) trained by us on LIBERO-Goal with observation dropout.
  It is the small model on which every operator is first debugged (minutes per experiment), and it is
  the substrate where the "no VLM" ablation lives.
- **Unconditional / null branch.** pi0.5 conditions on the prefix (images + language); a null branch
  exists only if the fine-tune used dropout (openpi configs to be checked). If not, prototypes are
  discovered conditionally (per observation cluster / per task prefix) and, for the motor-vocabulary
  view, under a fixed neutral prefix; the DP control is trained with dropout so both views exist there.
- **Benchmark: LIBERO** (Goal first, then Object / Spatial / 10), the lifelong protocol (sequential tasks,
  per-task success after each task, forward / backward transfer, AUC), 20-50 rollouts per task from the
  benchmark's initial states, headless (MUJOCO_GL=egl).
- **Compute.** A40 48 GB: pi0.5 inference in bf16 fits; adapters / LoRA fine-tuning of the action expert
  fits with openpi's low-memory configs; the VLM is never trained. JAX vs PyTorch openpi: whichever runs
  on this SLURM cluster without Docker (Appendix A). Nothing robotic is installed here yet.

## 4. The mechanism in chunk space

**Prototypes (discovery, "isolating agent prototypes").** For a set of demo chunks a (and their
observations o), at each noise level u on a grid: noise a to u, ascend the policy's score (Adam, Tweedie
mode), fit the Laplace extent; dedup / cluster per level; link parents by the coarser level's
Gaussians; counts = basin sizes. Two views: unconditional (null observation) = the motor vocabulary;
conditional = prototypes within an observation cluster. Validation of "isolated": (i) recurrence: the
prototype x task incidence matrix from assigning every demo chunk of every task to its nearest
prototype at each level (sparse, shared columns); (ii) semantics: prototypes align with gripper events
and phase boundaries of the demos better than a random segmentation; (iii) hierarchy: Zekun's
symmetric-KL-across-levels and normalised-displacement curves reproduce in chunk space; (iv) coverage:
sampling the policy through one prototype's Gaussian as the conditional branch (Eq. 14-16) executes
that motion in simulation.

**Codes and composition ("finding ways to do composition").** A task is a sequence over prototypes.
Three composition operators, to be compared:
- C-a  per-chunk product of experts (Zekun's operator): decompose each chunk of ONE demonstration into
       K prototypes per level; at rollout, the code for the current step is the code of the nearest
       demo step in observation-feature space (or a tiny retrieval over the demo's chunks); the
       policy samples through the PoE teacher as the conditional branch. Zero weights.
- C-b  temporal composition: the sequence of prototype ids over the demo is the program; execution
       advances along it by phase (the responsibility of the next prototype rising), the PoE at each
       phase from that prototype alone. This is the Cobweb-of-chunks view (the TRELLIS line).
- C-c  score composition across observation factors: separate conditionals for object / goal / scene
       (if the policy is trained factored) composed by score addition (Liu et al.), the Bradley /
       Nakkiran projective-composition regime; only if the substrate exposes factored conditioning.
Baselines: zero-shot conditioning on the new task's observations; few-shot fine-tuning on the one demo
(dense and LoRA); nearest-task retrieval; the unconditional prior.

**Address, fold, consolidate.** As in the image plan, in chunk space: a demo chunk explained by the
library (best-node NLL within the held-out calibration at every band level) stores a code (bytes: K ids
+ weights); an unexplained chunk creates a branch of nodes from the coarsest unexplained level down
and a gated expert on the action denoiser (KeyedLoRA on Conv1d/Linear, FiLM on GroupNorm channels, or a
prototype token through the observation-conditioning path), trained with the r ~ 1 arrival term and
the r ~ 0 anchor term on the policy's own samples of other prototypes (targets from the same policy
with the expert off: no teacher copy). Stop: S1 samples from the forced gate are claimed by the node in
chunk space, S2 leak and drift on old prototypes, S3 the denoising loss on held-back demo chunks at the
trained level, plus success in simulation for the folded task. Consolidate at the accrual trigger.

**The gate in chunk space.** Responsibility over a level's nodes with per-dim extents; chunks are 112-D
and smooth, so Gaussian prototypes are far better behaved than 3072-D pixels; the in-hull problem
(a new motion inside the hull of old ones) is now the CASE FOR composition, not a failure of the read.

## 6. Baselines and metrics

Metrics: success rate per task (LIBERO protocol), FWT / BWT / AUC over the stream, bytes charged per
task (`accounting.charge`: experts, nodes, codes, stored demos or samples, teacher copies), depth of
novelty per task, prototype-task incidence density, and for composition the success from ONE demo.
Baselines run: zero-shot, few-shot dense, few-shot LoRA, retrieval, ER, EWC, PackNet, multitask,
TAIL (LoRA per task), LOTUS (skill library), Sparse Diffusion Policy (MoE experts), flat LoRA per task
at equal bytes. Cited: Factorized Diffusion Policy, IsCiL, PoCo / GPC (test-time policy composition),
Decision Diffuser, CoRDE, DMPEL, CLARE-VLA (Appendix A carries the verified references).

## 8. Risks

- Simulation install on a cluster without root (EGL, mujoco, robosuite version pins): M0's kill.
- pi0.5 on this cluster: openpi's JAX/PyTorch stack, 3B-parameter inference and the LIBERO client-server
  example must run under SLURM without Docker; score ascent through a 300M action expert with a cached
  VLM prefix costs a forward per step per chain (budget the discovery: chains x levels x steps).
- pi0.7: may not be open; the plan does not depend on it.
- The conditional action distribution of demos may be nearly unimodal per observation, so prototypes
  must be read from the unconditional prior or across observation clusters; discovery may return
  per-demo modes (M2's kill).
- Composition by PoE over chunk Gaussians could produce off-manifold chunks (Zekun's App. J analogue:
  interpolation only); C-b and the projective-composition conditions are the fallbacks.
- Time: a trained DP per suite is 1-2 A40-days; a released checkpoint or dataset in a ready format
  saves most of it (Appendix A).
