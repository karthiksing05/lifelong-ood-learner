# Validity of the composition results

Written for: collaborators checking whether the embodied claims hold.  Generated by `experiments/make_validity_doc.py` from `results/robot/m3_stats.json` and the ledger rows named below; rerun it after new rows land.

## Protocol

- Held-out tasks: 23 LIBERO-90 tasks pi05_libero never saw; the first six chosen during development, the rest drawn by `random.Random(0)` from the unique-language tasks the prior fails zero-shot (ledger `m3_prereg`, tag wave_a).
- ONE demonstration per task (IPEC episode 0 of the task's text).  Every method first practises the prior for 10 episodes (init states 0-9) and keeps it where at least half succeed; methods are scored on init states 30-49, 20 episodes each, which no development run touched.
- Bytes are recomputed from the artifacts for every arm (`experiments/m34_report.arm_bytes`).  A replayed demo is charged its T x 7 action sequence (corrected 2026-09-26: earlier rows charged a full chunk per frame, about five times too much).
- Statistics (`lifelong_ood/stats.py`): Wilson intervals per arm; pooled rates and paired differences with a two-level bootstrap (tasks, then init states, the same states for both arms); the exact sign test on discordant pairs.  Pre-registered before the wave results: non-inferiority to the LoRA at a margin of 0.10 (one-sided 95% lower bound of the pooled paired difference), superiority to zero-shot, non-inferiority to replay; the practice-allocation rule (ledger `m3_prereg`, tag alloc).

Tasks where the prior's practice fails: 21 of 23; kept prior on 19, 67.

## Reliability

- Test-retest: the same (task, arm) on two disjoint init-state sets (10-29 and 30-49) over 22 cells: Pearson r = 0.958, mean absolute difference 0.073.
- A second demonstration (IPEC episode 1) on tasks 35, 36, 39, 42, same init states: replay 32/80 with demo 0, 58/80 with demo 1; borrowing (+ fold where it grasps) 34/80 with demo 0, 63/80 with demo 1; few-shot LoRA 34/80 with demo 0, 53/80 with demo 1; segment borrowing 33/80 with demo 0, 59/80 with demo 1.
- Forced execution (M2 validation iv, run retroactively; `ledger/r12_forced`): the 20 most-shared t = 0.4 prototypes executed open-loop in 5 scenes x 2 init states; the 16 that command at least 1.5 cm realise a median fraction 1.05 of the commanded displacement, cosine 0.998.  Kill (< 0.5): not triggered.
- EXPLORATORY (not pre-registered): the library's novelty read (fraction of the demo's frames it leaves unexplained) against success over 21 tasks: borrowing + fold Spearman +0.01 (permutation p 0.986), LoRA +0.08 (p 0.736), their difference -0.19 (p 0.418).

## What composition borrows

  Lenders: 0.89 / 0.10 / 0.01 of the borrowed pairs from t = 0.2 / 0.3 / 0.4; 0.95 from prototypes with at most 3 member frames, 0.99 from single-task prototypes, 0.01 from prototypes that at least 3 tasks share.
