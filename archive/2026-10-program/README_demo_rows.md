# Demo-based results (archived 2026-10-01)

These results use a demonstration of the task they score: the one-demo program (its guide replaced the policy's prediction,
and running the plan with no policy scored the same), per-task LoRAs, STRAP, DSRL on a LoRA base, experience replay, and
factor tokens fitted on the demo. By the user's decision of 2026-10-01 they are not tests of the thesis (a new task arrives
with no demonstration) and are not presented as results or baselines. Their ledger rows stay, and
`experiments/audit_numbers.py` still re-derives every number here.
